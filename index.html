The Application uses Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies to enhance user experience and provide certain features. The AI components may process user data to deliver personalized content, recommendations, or automated functionalities. All AI processing is performed in accordance with this privacy policy and applicable laws.

The Service Provider may use the information you provided to contact you from time to time to provide you with important information, required notices and marketing promotions.

For a better experience, while using the Application, the Service Provider may require you to provide certain personally identifiable information, including but not limited to email, name, userId, device sensor data, location, and camera images. Authentication is handled via Sign in with Apple and Sign in with Google. The Application never stores passwords. Your email and name are provided by Apple or Google at the time of account creation. The information that the Service Provider requests will be retained by them and used as described in this privacy policy.

Sensor Data

When you capture photos in ImageHunt, we may collect device sensor readings including accelerometer, gyroscope, magnetometer, and LiDAR depth data alongside your photos. This data is stored securely and used to enhance app functionality and future features. Location data within sensor readings is only collected if you have granted location permission and enabled location capture in Settings. Location data collection is optional and can be enabled or disabled at any time in the Application's Settings. The Application will function fully without location access.

Account Deletion

You may delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the app via Settings → Delete Account. Upon deletion, all personal information, hunt history, photos, and sensor data will be permanently removed from our servers within 30 days.
